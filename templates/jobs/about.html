About Remote Impact

Find meaningful work with better evidence.

Remote Impact is an independent job discovery service for people who want their work to improve lives, communities, or the planet.

What we do

We organize remote roles across climate, global health, AI safety, humanitarian work, education, and other social-impact fields. Each listing links back to its source so you can check the employer's requirements and apply in the right place.

How the catalogue works

  • We collect public listings from employer career pages and selected public feeds.
  • We record source links and checks so a role can be reviewed against the evidence available at that time.
  • We remove or update listings when they no longer meet our availability rules.
  • Counts and availability change as employers update their own sites; a listing is a useful lead, not a hiring guarantee.

Read the full listing-check method or browse current roles.

Who it is for

Remote Impact is built for job seekers who do not want access to meaningful work to depend on insider networks. It is also a starting point for employers and communities that want their public opportunities to be easier to discover.

Data and responsible use

We use public job evidence and the information people choose to submit to provide search, alerts, and career tools. Some tools use AI providers to interpret a brief or generate requested writing. Read our privacy policy before submitting personal or sensitive information.

Talk to us

For corrections, partnerships, employer questions, or accessibility feedback, contact Remote Impact.
