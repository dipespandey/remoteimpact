How to Access

Organizations log in to Remote Impact and access Hire for Mission through the dashboard:

https://remoteimpact.org/hire-for-mission/dashboard/

After login, post a job, generate AI screening questions, receive applications with screening answers, and view candidate shortlists with AI scores.

Getting Started in 7 Steps

  1. Log in to Remote Impact as an organization admin
  2. Post a job (or select an existing job)
  3. Click "Generate Screening Questions" → AI creates 3-5 questions in 30 seconds
  4. Candidates apply with resume + screening answers
  5. View shortlist of top 15-20 candidates sorted by AI score
  6. Review details → See answers, team feedback, AI reasoning
  7. Take action → Invite to interview, reject, schedule call
