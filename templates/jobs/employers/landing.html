Questions

How do I pay?
Once, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Stripe sends a receipt and an invoice for your records.
Does paying change AI Job Scout matches?
No. Featured roles are pinned and labelled where people browse, but Scout matching is based on fit only.
How do I see results?
A private link shows views, apply clicks and Scout matches for each live role.
Do you guarantee applicants?
No. We guarantee placement and transparent numbers, not outcomes. If something is not right, email us and we will sort it out.
Can I change a posted role?
Yes. Email hello@remoteimpact.org with the change and we will update it within one business day.
What do you remove?
Roles that are not remote, are not real openings, or ask candidates to pay. Paid listings that break these rules are refunded and removed.
Do job seekers pay to apply?
Never. Applying and expressing interest are always free.
