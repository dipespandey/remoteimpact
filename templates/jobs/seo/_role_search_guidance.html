Choosing a remote program manager role

Program manager, project manager or program director?

Read the responsibilities alongside the title. A program manager may coordinate several workstreams, manage partners and report on outcomes. A program director vacancy may add budget ownership, staff management or responsibility for the overall strategy. This page includes program manager, program lead and program director titles; the employer's description determines the actual scope.

For work focused on a defined delivery schedule, compare remote project manager jobs. For responsibility across internal systems and team processes, compare remote operations manager jobs.

Check delivery requirements before applying

Look for required country residence, working-hour overlap, travel and field visits. Remote program management can still involve meetings with local partners or travel to delivery sites. Check whether the role is employment or a consultancy, which budget you would manage, and whether grant reporting or sector experience is mandatory. An undisclosed salary or an unspecified hiring country needs confirmation from the employer.

Show evidence of running a program

Choose an example that explains the goal, your responsibility, the partners involved and how you tracked progress. Describe a delivery problem, the decision you made and the result you can substantiate. Separate your contribution from the team's work, and use figures only when you can share them. Match this evidence to the vacancy's duties rather than relying on the job title alone.

Choosing a remote communications manager role

Find the audience and the purpose of the role

Compare the actual work: media relations, research communications, public campaigns, internal communications or donor updates. A combined development and communications role may include fundraising responsibilities. A director or head-of-communications vacancy may involve managing a team and setting strategy. Read those duties before deciding whether the title matches your experience.

If the work centers on acquisition, campaigns and audience growth, also compare remote marketing manager jobs. For a sector change, use the impact career guides to understand the organizations and their work.

Check language, time zones and public-facing duties

Remote does not automatically mean worldwide. Confirm the hiring countries and working hours, plus any travel, event attendance or out-of-hours media response. Check which languages you must write in and whether the employer requires existing press relationships or experience communicating specialist research. Missing requirements should remain questions for the employer.

Make your portfolio relevant to the vacancy

Select a few samples that match the employer's audience and channels. For each, explain the brief, your contribution, the editorial choices and the outcome you can evidence. Distinguish reach from results such as registrations or donations, and do not claim attribution you cannot support. Use public or permission-cleared work; describe confidential projects without sharing the material.
