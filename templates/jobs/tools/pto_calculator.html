Frequently Asked Questions

How many work days are in a year?

A standard year has 260 work days (52 weeks × 5 weekdays). After removing 11 US federal holidays, you get 249 working days. Leap years may vary by one or two days depending on weekend alignment.

How is PTO accrual calculated?

Divide your annual PTO by the number of pay periods: 26 for biweekly or 12 for monthly. For example, 15 annual PTO days on a biweekly schedule accrues ~0.58 days per pay period.

Which US federal holidays are excluded?

New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day — 11 holidays total.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
