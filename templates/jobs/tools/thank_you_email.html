5 Rules for Post-Interview Thank You Emails

  1. Send within 24 hours: Timing matters. Send your thank you email the same day or by the next morning while the conversation is still fresh in everyone's mind.
  2. Reference something specific: Mention a topic, project, or insight from the interview. This proves you were engaged and helps the interviewer remember you.
  3. Reaffirm your interest: Clearly state that you're excited about the role and the company. Enthusiasm is a signal that you'll accept an offer.
  4. Keep it concise: Three to four short paragraphs is ideal. Your interviewer is busy — respect their time with a focused, scannable email.
  5. Proofread twice: Typos in a thank you email can undo a great interview. Read it aloud, then read it again before hitting send.
