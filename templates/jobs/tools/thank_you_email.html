Five rules for post-interview thank-you emails

  1. Send within 24 hours. Send it the same day or the next morning, while the conversation is still fresh.
  2. Mention something specific. A topic, project or idea from the interview shows you were listening and helps the interviewer remember you.
  3. Restate your interest. Say plainly that you want the role and why. It tells them you're likely to accept an offer.
  4. Keep it short. Three or four short paragraphs. Your interviewer is busy.
  5. Proofread twice. A typo can undo a good interview. Read it aloud, then read it again before you send it.
