What a good resignation letter does

A resignation letter is a short record that you're leaving and when. Whether you're moving to a new role, relocating or changing careers, a clear, polite letter helps you leave on good terms. This generator uses your role, company, reason and tone to write a first draft you can adjust.

  • Keep it short. One page or less is enough.
  • Stay professional. Whatever your reasons for leaving, keep the tone positive and respectful.
  • State your last day. Give a clear date, usually two weeks from now, or whatever your contract requires.
  • Say thank you. Thank your manager and the team for what you've learned.
  • Offer to help with the handover. Mention training a replacement or documenting your work.
