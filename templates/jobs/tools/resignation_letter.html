Why Use a Resignation Letter Generator?

Writing a resignation letter can feel daunting. Whether you're leaving for a new opportunity, relocating, or making a career change, a well-crafted resignation letter ensures you leave on good terms and maintain your professional reputation.

Our AI-powered resignation letter generator creates a personalized, polished letter in seconds. It considers your specific situation — your role, company, reason for leaving, and preferred tone — to produce a letter that's ready to customize and send.

Tips for a Great Resignation Letter

  • Keep it concise: A resignation letter should be brief and to the point — ideally one page or less.
  • Be professional: Regardless of your reasons for leaving, maintain a positive and respectful tone.
  • State your last day: Give clear notice of when you'll be leaving, typically two weeks.
  • Express gratitude: Thank your manager and the company for the opportunities you've had.
  • Offer to help transition: Show willingness to train your replacement or hand off responsibilities.
