How to Prepare for Remote Job Interviews

Test Your Technology

Check your camera, microphone, internet connection, and the video platform at least 24 hours before the interview.

Research the Organization

Understand their mission, recent projects, impact metrics, and culture. Tailor your answers to show alignment with their values.

Prepare STAR Stories

Structure your answers using Situation, Task, Action, Result. Have 5–7 stories ready that demonstrate different competencies.

Master On-Camera Presence

Look at the camera lens (not the screen), ensure front-facing light, use a clean background, and sit at eye level.

Prepare Your Own Questions

Have 3–5 thoughtful questions about remote work culture, team collaboration, growth opportunities, and impact measurement.

Follow Up Promptly

Send a personalized thank-you email within 24 hours referencing specific conversation points. It shows professionalism and genuine interest.
