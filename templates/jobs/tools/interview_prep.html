How to prepare for a remote interview

  • Test your setup a day early. Check camera, microphone, connection and the video platform at least 24 hours before.
  • Research the organization. Know their mission, recent projects and how they measure impact, and connect your answers to them.
  • Prepare STAR stories. Situation, Task, Action, Result. Have 5 to 7 stories ready that show different skills.
  • Look at the camera, not the screen. Light from the front, a plain background, and the camera at eye level.
  • Bring your own questions. Ask 3 to 5 about remote work habits, how the team collaborates, growth, and how impact is measured.
  • Follow up within 24 hours. A short thank-you email that mentions something specific from the conversation. The thank-you email tool can draft one.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare for a remote job interview?

Test your technology beforehand, choose a quiet well-lit space, research the organization's mission, prepare STAR-method stories, have questions ready for the interviewer, and dress professionally even on video.

What questions are commonly asked in nonprofit interviews?

Common nonprofit interview questions include: Why are you passionate about our mission? How do you handle working with limited resources? Describe your experience with stakeholder engagement. How do you measure impact?

How is a remote interview different from in-person?

Remote interviews require strong on-camera presence, reliable technology, a professional background, and the ability to build rapport virtually. You should also be prepared for asynchronous components and virtual assessments.

What are the best tips for video interview success?

Look at the camera (not the screen), ensure good lighting from the front, use a neutral background, speak clearly with pauses, keep notes nearby but don't read from them, and follow up with a thank-you email.

How many practice questions should I prepare for an interview?

Aim to prepare answers for at least 10–15 common questions in your field. Our AI generator creates 10 tailored questions per session covering behavioral, technical, culture fit, leadership, and remote work scenarios.
