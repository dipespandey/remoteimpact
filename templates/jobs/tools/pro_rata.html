How to read an FTE salary advert

Actual annual pay = annual FTE salary × your paid weekly hours ÷ full-time weekly hours. Monthly pay = actual annual pay ÷ 12. Contract total = monthly pay × paid months.

For example, 40,000 FTE at 21 hours out of a 35-hour week means 24,000 a year, averaging 2,000 a month. A six-month paid contract totals 12,000 gross.

If an advert already gives the actual part-time salary, do not pro-rate it again. Ask the employer whether the figure is FTE or actual, whether the term is fully paid, and how paid leave is handled.
