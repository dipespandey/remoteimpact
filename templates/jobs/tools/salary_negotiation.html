Seven salary negotiation tips for remote workers

  1. Research market rates. Use Glassdoor, Levels.fyi and Payscale to see what your role pays in your industry and location.
  2. Put numbers on your impact. Revenue generated, costs saved, people served, projects delivered.
  3. Negotiate the full package. Remote roles often include stipends for home office, internet, coworking, wellness and learning. Ask about them.
  4. Practice out loud. Rehearse with a friend or record yourself. Hearing your own words makes the real conversation easier.
  5. Anchor high, but realistic. Your first number sets the range. Start near the top of what your research supports so there's room to meet in the middle.
  6. Don't share your current salary first. In many places employers can't legally ask. Point to your target range and the market data behind it.
  7. Get it in writing. Once you agree verbally, ask for a revised offer letter before accepting. Remote terms such as location flexibility and time zone expectations should be written down too.
