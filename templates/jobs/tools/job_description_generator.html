How it works

Enter the job title and whatever you know about the role: responsibilities, skills, salary and the impact area your organization works in. The generator writes a draft with an overview, an about-us placeholder, responsibilities, qualifications, benefits and how to apply. Review it, add your own voice and benefits, and post it wherever you hire.

  • Saves drafting time. You start from a complete draft instead of a blank page.
  • Inclusive wording. The draft avoids hard "must have X years" requirements and splits required from nice-to-have skills, which helps you reach a wider pool of candidates.
  • Written for impact roles. It connects the role to your mission and the impact area you choose.
