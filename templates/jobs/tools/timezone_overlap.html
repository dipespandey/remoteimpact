How to Use the Time Zone Overlap Finder

  1. Select 2 to 4 time zones where your team members are located.
  2. Adjust the working hours to match your team's schedule (default is 9 AM – 5 PM).
  3. View the visual chart to see which hours overlap across all selected zones.
  4. The green highlighted window shows the best time for team meetings.

Why Overlapping Hours Matter for Remote Teams

Distributed teams that span multiple time zones face a common challenge: finding enough shared working hours for synchronous collaboration. Research shows that teams with at least 3-4 overlapping hours maintain stronger communication and faster decision-making.

This free tool helps remote-first companies, freelancers, and globally distributed teams quickly identify the best windows for meetings, pair programming, and real-time collaboration.
