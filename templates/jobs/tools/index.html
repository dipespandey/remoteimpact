Free tools for remote job seekers

Drafts, calculators and checklists for applying, negotiating and working remotely. Most need no account.

Applying and interviewing

  • Cover letter generator Paste a job post or link and your highlights to get a first-draft cover letter or interview questions for that job. Free account needed.
  • Interview questions Ten likely questions for a job title and type of organization, with an outline for each answer.
  • Thank-you email Draft a short follow-up to send after an interview.
  • Skills gap analyzer Compare your resume with a job description to see matching, missing and transferable skills.
  • Word counter Count words, characters and reading time for cover letters and application answers.
  • Resignation letter Write a clear, professional letter to leave your current job.

Pay and offers

  • Salary negotiation script A script with talking points and replies to common objections, for a new offer or a raise.
  • Salary to hourly calculator Convert an annual salary to an hourly rate and back.
  • Pay raise calculator See what a raise adds per year, month and paycheck.
  • Freelance rate calculator Work out an hourly rate from your income goal, costs and billable hours.
  • Cost of living comparison Compare what the same salary covers in two cities.

Working remotely

  • Remote work readiness quiz A short quiz on how ready you are for remote work, with tips for the weaker areas.
  • Time zone overlap finder Find shared working hours across two to four time zones.
  • PTO and work days calculator Count work days between dates and track paid time off.
  • Pomodoro timer A focus timer with adjustable work and break lengths.

For hiring teams

  • Job description generator Draft an inclusive job description for a remote role at an impact organization.
