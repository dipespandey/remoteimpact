Questions

What does the Remote Work Readiness Quiz measure?

It covers 12 areas, including self-discipline, home office setup, written and async communication, technical setup, motivation and work-life boundaries. You get a score out of 48 and tips for the areas that scored low.

How long does the assessment take?

About 2–3 minutes. There are 12 multiple-choice questions and the result shows as soon as you answer the last one.

What score do I need to be ready for remote work?

40–48: remote work natural. 30–39: almost ready. 20–29: needs preparation. Below 20: consider hybrid first.

Is this quiz free and private?

Yes. It is free, runs in your browser and does not send your answers to our server. You do not need an account.

Can I retake the quiz?

Yes. Retake it as often as you like to see how your setup and habits change.
