About this data

  • Source. Currently available job listings on Remote Impact with evidence supporting both a minimum and a maximum annual USD salary ({{ total_with_salary|intcomma }} right now). Listings with a maximum of 1,000,000 or more are left out as outliers.
  • Currency. Only supported annual USD amounts are included. Other currencies, hourly pay, fixed-term totals and unsupported estimates are excluded; no currency conversion is applied.
  • Coverage. Many employers do not publish comparable pay, so these figures cover only part of the listings. Each listing has equal weight; averages are not medians or a representative market survey. Actual offers vary with experience, location and organization.
