What this means for your search

Unknown eligibility is not worldwide eligibility. Missing pay is not zero pay. An amount stored without enough evidence for its currency and period should not be compared with an annual salary target.

These figures describe Remote Impact’s available catalogue at collection time. They do not measure all remote jobs, prove that an employer is still hiring, or establish work authorization. Our API and MCP tools expose uncertainty so assistants can flag it instead of guessing.
