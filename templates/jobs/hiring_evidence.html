Method and limitations

Remote Impact is the publisher of this analysis. Counts use the same availability rules as our public job board. Sector snapshots filter by catalogue impact category; the early-career snapshot filters entry and internship experience labels. One employer can have several listings, including region variants. This is a sample of sources we track, not all vacancies, unique openings, applicant competition, or hiring outcomes. The seven-day measure uses posting dates, not first discovery dates. Fields may be extracted automatically and can be incomplete or incorrect.

These figures update as the catalogue changes; they do not establish a trend. Read how listings are checked, report a correction, or see the whole-catalogue snapshot.
