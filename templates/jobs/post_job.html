Questions

When does my listing go live?

As soon as payment completes. We remove and refund listings that aren't remote, aren't real openings, or ask candidates to pay.

What if I need to edit my post?

Email hello@remoteimpact.org with the change and we'll update it within one business day.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

If you are hiring for three or more roles, email hello@remoteimpact.org and we will quote a package.
