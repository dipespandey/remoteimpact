    What are humanitarian and INGO careers?

    Humanitarian careers encompass any work that responds to, prepares for, or helps communities recover from crises — armed conflict, natural disasters, famine, displacement. INGO careers more broadly include development work aimed at long-term poverty reduction, governance strengthening, and capacity building.

    Roles that may permit remote work include grant management, donor relations, monitoring and evaluation, communications, data analysis, information management and technology. Programme and advisory roles can involve travel or a required duty station. A remote label alone does not establish that applicants can work from any country.

    Start with current humanitarian listings or the broader remote nonprofit jobs search. Check the original posting for application status, location, travel and work authorization. The job board's counts use its availability rules; they are not a guarantee that applications remain open.

    Key skills employers look for

    • Programme & project management — Logical frameworks, results-based management, donor reporting (ECHO, FCDO and UN agency formats).
    • Monitoring, evaluation & learning (MEL) — Survey design, qualitative methods, data visualisation, DHIS2/KoboToolbox.
    • Grant writing & proposal development — Crafting competitive bids for institutional donors.
    • Languages — French, Arabic and Spanish widen the roles you can apply for.
    • Information management — Humanitarian data standards (HDX, IATI), GIS mapping, dashboard creation.
    • Protection & safeguarding — Understanding of humanitarian principles, do-no-harm frameworks, and child protection policies.

    How to get started

    If you lack field experience, look for roles where your existing skills apply, such as communications, finance, operations or data. Domestic volunteering with refugee or disaster-response organizations can provide relevant experience. Compare current internships and fellowships, and check whether an employer requires a degree, language skills, field experience or a particular work location before applying.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I get a remote INGO job without field experience?

    Some support functions, including grant administration, communications, data, HR and IT, may accept applicants without field experience. Requirements vary by employer and role. Read the original posting before assuming a role is fully remote or open in your country.

    Where can I find current INGO jobs?

    Browse humanitarian jobs on Remote Impact, then confirm each opening on the employer's own careers page. International organizations use different labels for remote, home-based, consultancy and duty-station roles; check the actual contract and location terms.

    What skills do humanitarian employers value most?

    Skills depend on the role. Programme management, monitoring and evaluation, grant writing, logistics, protection, languages, GIS and data collection can be relevant. Match the specific requirements in each vacancy rather than treating this as a universal checklist.

    How much do remote humanitarian jobs pay?

    Pay varies by employer, contract, duty station and seniority. We show advertised compensation when a source provides it; a missing figure is unknown, not unpaid. Compare the figures on current humanitarian listings and confirm the terms on the original posting.
