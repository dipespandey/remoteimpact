    Career tracks in education

    Education careers span nonprofits, venture-backed startups, and corporate learning. Remote work is common across all three.

    • Nonprofit education — Programme managers, curriculum developers, and education policy analysts at organisations like Teach For All, KIPP, and education foundations.
    • Edtech startups — Product managers, engineers, content creators, and learning designers at companies building educational technology.
    • Corporate L&D — Learning and development specialists, instructional designers, and training managers building employee education programmes.
    • Content and curriculum — Subject matter experts, content writers, and curriculum designers creating educational materials and courses.

    Key skills employers want

    Teaching and tech: Edtech companies value educators who can code and technologists who understand pedagogy. Candidates who combine both are well placed.

    • Instructional design — ADDIE, SAM, backwards design. Tools: Articulate, Captivate, Rise. Learning objectives, assessment design, accessibility (WCAG).
    • Learning science — Cognitive load theory, spaced repetition, gamification, personalised learning paths, data-informed curriculum iteration.
    • Education data — Learning analytics, A/B testing educational interventions, outcomes measurement, student success metrics.
    • UX for education — User research with learners, accessibility, engagement design, mobile-first learning experiences.

    Education employers to research

    Khan Academy

    Nonprofit offering free online education. Its careers board includes remote positions with explicit country restrictions as well as location-based roles. Check the individual posting before applying.

    Type: nonprofit. Eligibility: role-specific; remote does not mean worldwide.

    Check Khan Academy’s current openings and hiring locations.

    Duolingo

    Language-learning app and public company (DUOL). Do not assume its roles are remote: check the work arrangement and office requirements on each posting.

    Focus: research, engineering and language learning. Check Duolingo’s careers page for current arrangements.

    Coursera

    Online learning platform that partners with universities and companies. Public company (COUR) offering degrees, certificates, and enterprise learning, with remote roles in product, content partnerships, and engineering.

    Type: B2B and B2C, remote-friendly.

    Teach For All

    Global network of independent partner organisations working to expand educational opportunity. Remote roles in network support, knowledge management, and programme design.

    Type: nonprofit network. Roles: programme and network support.

    Outschool

    Marketplace for live online classes for children aged 3–18, connecting independent educators with learners. Remote-first, hiring for product, engineering, and educator success.

    Type: startup, remote-first.

    Also worth looking at: 2U/edX, Udemy, Skillshare, Brilliant, Age of Learning (ABCmouse), Carnegie Learning, Newsela, and education-focused foundations such as the Gates Foundation, the Walton Family Foundation, and the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need a teaching background for edtech jobs?

    Not always. Product, engineering, and marketing roles at edtech companies often prioritise tech skills over classroom experience. However, instructional design, curriculum development, and learning science roles typically prefer candidates with education backgrounds. Former teachers are valued for content roles and user research.

    What's the salary range for remote education jobs?

    Pay depends on the employer, hiring country, role, seniority and contract. Compare the original listing’s currency, pay period and benefits. A salary advertised for one country or role is not a benchmark for every remote education job. If pay is not listed, ask the employer before committing to an application process.

    Which edtech companies are fully remote?

    Work arrangements vary by employer and role. Khan Academy’s careers board includes remote positions with country restrictions and location-based roles. Check each employer’s current posting for eligible countries, office attendance and work authorization; do not assume a company hires remotely worldwide.
