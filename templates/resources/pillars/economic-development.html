    Career tracks in economic development

    • Multilateral development banks — World Bank Group, IFC, ADB, AfDB. Economists, operations officers, investment analysts. Competitive salaries with tax-free status.
    • Bilateral aid agencies — FCDO, GIZ, JICA. Programme officers, contracting specialists, technical advisors. Mix of headquarters and field roles.
    • Impact investing — DFIs, impact funds, ESG investing. Investment analysts, portfolio managers, impact measurement specialists.
    • Development consulting — DAI, Chemonics, Palladium, Abt. Project managers, M&E specialists, sector experts. Frequent travel, with some remote options.

    Key skills for development careers

    • Econometrics and impact evaluation — RCTs, quasi-experimental methods, Stata/R, cost-benefit analysis, J-PAL/IPA methodology.
    • Programme design and M&E — Theory of change, logframes, MEAL frameworks, adaptive management, results-based financing.
    • Stakeholder management — Government relations, donor coordination, community engagement, political economy analysis.
    • Sector expertise — Agriculture, financial inclusion, infrastructure, governance, gender. Deep specialisation is valued.

    Employers hiring

    World Bank Group

    Multilateral development institution hiring economists, operations officers, and investment analysts. The Young Professionals Program is the main entry track. Based in Washington, DC, with some remote flexibility.

    Type: multilateral. Pay: $80K–200K (tax-free). Entry: Young Professionals Program.

    IFC (International Finance Corporation)

    The World Bank Group's private-sector arm. Hires investment officers, ESG specialists, and portfolio managers to work on private equity and debt in emerging markets. A strong finance background is required.

    Focus: private sector, finance, emerging markets.

    Chemonics, DAI, and Palladium

    Large development consulting firms that implement donor-funded projects. Hire project managers, M&E specialists, and sector experts, with a mix of home-office and field roles.

    Type: consulting. Pay: $70K–150K. Travel required.

    Also hiring: Mastercard Foundation, Gates Foundation, Omidyar Network, Acumen, Root Capital, Kiva, CGAP, and financial-inclusion fintechs like Branch, Wave, and M-KOPA.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need a master's degree for development jobs?

    For most multilateral and consulting roles, yes. A master's in economics, public policy, international development, or a relevant technical field (public health, agriculture, etc.) is strongly preferred. Some entry-level and operations roles accept bachelor's degrees with relevant experience.

    Are development jobs remote-friendly?

    Increasingly. Since COVID, multilaterals and consulting firms have expanded remote and hybrid options. Short-term consultancies are often fully remote, while field-based roles still require in-country presence. Home-office roles at consulting firms (M&E, technical writing, research) are often remote-friendly.
