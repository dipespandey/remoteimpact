    Career tracks in mental health

    Mental health careers span clinical practice, nonprofit work and technology. Wider use of telehealth since the pandemic has opened remote options at most levels.

    • Clinical therapy — Licensed therapists providing individual, couples or group therapy via telehealth. Requires LCSW, LPC, LMFT or doctoral credentials.
    • Nonprofit mental health — Programme managers, outreach coordinators and peer support specialists at mental health nonprofits and community health centres.
    • Mental health tech — Product managers, engineers and designers building apps for meditation, therapy matching and digital therapeutics.
    • Corporate wellness — Employee wellness programme managers, EAP coordinators and mental health benefits specialists at large employers.

    Key skills and credentials

    Clinical vs. non-clinical. Clinical requirements depend on the profession and jurisdiction; check the licensing board’s current education, supervised-practice and authorization requirements. Non-clinical roles value lived experience, programme management skills and comfort discussing mental health.

    • Clinical licensure — The relevant professional license and practice authorization, including any separate compact or telehealth-registration requirements. A degree or home-state license alone does not guarantee cross-state authorization.
    • Peer support certification — Certified Peer Specialist (CPS) credentials for roles supporting people with lived mental health experience.
    • Health coaching — NBC-HWC or similar certifications for wellness coaching roles that don't require clinical licensure.
    • Programme management — Grant management, outcomes measurement and community partnership skills for nonprofit leadership roles.

    Employers hiring remotely

    Lyra Health

    Enterprise mental health platform that employers offer as a staff benefit. Hires remote therapists (W-2), clinical directors, and product and engineering staff.

    Example checked 28 October 2026: Lyra’s full-time telehealth therapist posting lists $70,000–$92,000 USD annual base pay, U.S. remote work and independent clinical licensure. This is one vacancy, not a platform-wide salary promise.

    BetterHelp (Teladoc)

    Online therapy platform using independent providers. Its application requirements include independent clinical licensure, prior therapy experience and U.S. residence; it does not accept providers who need supervision.

    Check BetterHelp’s provider requirements and the compensation terms offered for your location and services. Do not compare a contractor session rate directly with an employee salary.

    Headspace

    Meditation app combined with coaching and clinical services. Hires coaches, therapists, psychiatrists and tech staff.

    Focus: app and clinical care, tech roles. Location: remote-first.

    NAMI (National Alliance on Mental Illness)

    Grassroots mental health organisation in the US. Hires for advocacy, peer support programmes, education and state affiliate coordination.

    Focus: nonprofit advocacy, peer support.

    Also hiring: Modern Health, Spring Health, Talkspace, Alma, Two Chairs, Cerebral and Brightside.

    U.S. licensing sources: HHS’s licensure overview and cross-state practice guidance. Other countries have their own professional and telehealth rules.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can therapists work fully remote?

    Some employers offer telehealth roles, but remote work does not remove professional authorization or location requirements. In the U.S., clinicians must meet requirements where they are located and be licensed or legally permitted to practise where the patient is located. Check the relevant boards and employer; interstate compacts and telehealth registration have their own conditions.

    What credentials do I need for mental health jobs?

    Clinical roles require the professional license and practice authorization relevant to the role and jurisdiction; a degree alone does not establish permission to practise. Nonclinical programme, operations and technology roles have their own vacancy-specific requirements. Check the employer’s requirements and the appropriate licensing board for clinical work.

    What's the salary range for remote mental health jobs?

    Compare the advertised offer for the specific role and location. Employee annual salary, contractor live-session compensation and a private practice’s client fee are different measures. Check paid administrative time, expected caseload, benefits, expenses and required licensure before comparing offers; one platform’s rate is not a market salary range.
