    • {% endblock %} {% block guide_title %}Health-tech organisations working in global health{% endblock %} {% block guide_lede %}These organisations build products for health systems and patients, including in low- and middle-income countries, and several of them hire remote teams across engineering, product, data and operations.{% endblock %} {% block guide_body %}

    1. Dimagi

    Dimagi builds CommCare, an open-source mobile platform used by frontline health workers for data collection, case management, and decision support. Dimagi is one of the longer-established names in digital health for development. They hire remotely for software engineering, data science, project management, and technical consulting roles.

    2. Medic

    Medic develops the Community Health Toolkit (CHT), an open-source platform that powers community health systems in sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia. Their tools help community health workers register patients, track pregnancies, and deliver care protocols through basic mobile phones. Medic hires remote software engineers, product designers, and technical programme managers who want to work at the intersection of open source and public health.

    3. Zipline

    Zipline operates the world's largest autonomous drone delivery network, transporting blood, vaccines, and medications to hard-to-reach clinics across Rwanda, Ghana, Nigeria, and beyond. It has since expanded beyond medical supplies. Zipline hires remotely for software engineering, flight operations, data analytics, and regulatory affairs positions.

    4. Ada Health

    Ada Health has built a symptom assessment app. The app helps users understand their symptoms and guides them to appropriate care, reducing unnecessary clinic visits while catching serious conditions early. Ada hires remotely for medical knowledge engineering, natural language processing research, and clinical validation roles.

    5. Eko

    Eko develops cardiac screening tools that use machine learning, including smart stethoscopes and ECG devices that detect heart disease at the point of care. Eko hires for hardware engineering, machine learning, clinical research, and sales roles, with several positions offering remote flexibility.

    Why work at a health-tech organisation?

    Compared to large UN agencies or traditional NGOs, health-tech startups offer faster feedback loops, and more ownership over your work. Check each organisation's careers page for current remote openings. For a broader view of the sector, see our global health careers guide.
