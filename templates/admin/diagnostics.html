How to read this report · Privacy & data coverage

{{ a.since|date:"M j, Y H:i" }} – {{ a.until|date:"M j, Y H:i" }} UTC. First and last chart days may be partial. Staff and configured internal accounts are excluded; anonymous automation may remain. Requests are not people, email acceptance is not inbox delivery, and clicks are not applications.

Activation counts new accounts once per stage. Email actions must follow Scout creation; recent accounts have had less time to activate. Scout outcomes prioritize accepted email, then empty, then failed, then unknown. A successful retry resolves a failed-send bucket.

Journey lists use a bounded recent-event sample. Selected trails show up to 100 events within the period. Account trails contain explicitly signed-in activity only; use related flow links for earlier anonymous Scout activity. Unlinked events cannot be reconstructed as sessions. No IP-based identity guesses. Pseudonyms are not verified people; links expire after 24 hours.

No replay, new cookies, fingerprinting, submitted briefs, email addresses or raw metadata. Views require event-view permissions and are never publicly cached. For verified cash, use Revenue operations.
