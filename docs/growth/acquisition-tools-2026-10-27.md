# Acquisition tools: human decisions and agent evidence

Research date: 27 October 2026. Decision: prioritise tools that help someone decide whether to apply or whether they can afford to accept. Build on our existing catalogue and Scout evidence, rather than duplicating a broad job board or calculator directory.

## What the evidence supports

The user-supplied query table contains 480 impressions and 93 clicks across four spellings of remote nonprofit jobs. These are row totals, not unique people, and the date, country, device and search-engine scope were not supplied. Education, climate, conservation and fellowship queries are additional observed themes. This supports relevance to our audience; it does not measure demand for a new calculator.

A read-only catalogue check on 27 October found 384 records in the nonprofit search, including 313 with a USA label and 54 with a Worldwide label. Only one of the records had an employer type explicitly recorded as nonprofit. The rest entered through source classification or name-based discovery, which must remain visible. These observations motivate publishing useful denominators and classification limits rather than implying that every record is a verified charity or globally eligible opening.

Keyword discovery was attempted with the registered SEO CLI report. It returned `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`: there is no connected DataForSEO, Semrush or Ahrefs provider. Search volumes, keyword difficulty and traffic forecasts are therefore **unknown**. The priorities below are product/acquisition hypotheses supported by current examples and competitor inspection, not volume-backed forecasts.

Google says ordinary SEO fundamentals apply to AI features, that important information should be textual, and that structured data should match the page. A special AI file or special schema does not secure inclusion: [Google's AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features). A public JSON endpoint is useful for consumers and reproducibility; it is not a citation-ranking trick.

## Ship in this order

| Priority | Tool and user question | Search / sharing hypothesis | Agent version | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Can I apply from my country?** Paste a posting, choose residence, and see explicit permission, conflicting restrictions and unknowns with exact quotes. | `can I apply for remote US jobs from [country]`, `remote job eligibility checker`; share in country-specific job communities. | `check_location_evidence(job_id, residence_country)` returns rule, quote, source URL, source-check time, evidence strength and unresolved restrictions. | Closest bridge to a relevant live shortlist, and addresses a failure we have seen in real Scout searches. Higher extraction/evaluation work than a calculator. |
| 2 | **What will this part-time job actually pay?** Convert an FTE advert into annual, monthly and contract-total gross pay using the employer's full-time hours and the actual hours/term. | `pro rata salary calculator`, `FTE salary calculator`, `actual part time salary`; useful on individual nonprofit job pages. | Deterministic `normalise_pay` with explicit currency, input period, hours, paid weeks and term; return formula and assumptions. | Very cheap to serve, immediate utility, relevant to nonprofit ads. Competitive head terms; win through job-linked inputs and clear ambiguity handling. |
| 3 | **Can I afford this fellowship?** Show total funding, monthly living-cost gap and maximum upfront cash needed before stipend/reimbursement arrives. | `fellowship stipend calculator`, `is [programme] fully funded`, `can I afford a fellowship`; useful to university advisers. | `fellowship_cashflow` takes dated payment/cost schedules, returns monthly balance and maximum funding gap. | Strong match to the mission of access beyond privileged applicants. Cash timing differentiates it from a generic stipend or cost-of-living calculator. Demand size still unverified. |
| 4 | **Is this really entry-level?** Highlight essential experience/credentials, preferences, transferable duties and unclear requirements. | `entry level job requires experience`, `nonprofit jobs no experience`; link to early-career searches. | `get_requirement_evidence(job_id)` separates required/preferred/unclear with quotes. | Uses existing source facts and Scout checks. Improve the current skills-gap tool rather than launching another opaque match score. Never predict interview or hiring probability. |
| 5 | **Which paid fellowships can I realistically apply to?** Filter citizenship, residence, education, career stage, remote/in-person and funding coverage. | Existing fellowship queries; university careers pages and research communities. | Read-only programme search with cohort deadline, source, checked time and unknown fields. | Extend the existing early-careers finder. This is chiefly a curation and freshness problem, not another directory template. |
| 6 | **When does this application close in my time zone?** Resolve an explicit employer deadline, show local time and export a calendar event. | `AoE deadline converter`, `[fellowship] deadline timezone`; shareable application reminders. | Deterministic time conversion returns IANA zone, UTC instant and ambiguity state. | Low running cost and useful companion feature. Generic time tools are well established, so embed on opportunities rather than rely on head-term rankings. |
| 7 | **What would an impact career move cost me?** Compare actual gross earnings, user-supplied costs and savings runway for two offers, including a short contract or unpaid gap. | `can I afford a pay cut`, `nonprofit job salary tradeoff`; career-change communities. | A calculation endpoint using user-provided assumptions; no fabricated tax or exchange-rate estimates. | Broader audience but less direct job intent. Extend existing salary/freelance tools and only build after measuring the first three. |
| 8 | **Is this job still listed, and what evidence changed?** Resolve our job ID to its listing state, last source check, source link and a bounded evidence diff. | Primarily agent/developer distribution and fewer dead application links, rather than SEO head terms. | Add to the existing REST/MCP surface; explicit `unknown` when not checked. | Useful differentiator if freshness can be maintained. A cache hit must never refresh the employer-check timestamp. No claim of employer acceptance from a feed sighting. |

The newly implemented **nonprofit location report and JSON aggregate** is the first small public evidence tool. It exposes counts and unknowns without collecting personal information or requiring a key. It is a foundation for credible references, not a replacement for the per-posting checker in priority 1.

## Competitors and the gap to test

| Area | Evidence inspected | Implication for Remote Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Remote eligibility | [Job Badger's free checker](https://job-badger.com/tools/remote-job-checker) already quotes country, timezone and authorisation restrictions and explains that no detected rule is not permission. | The idea is validated as a problem, not novel. Match or exceed quote quality, add impact-specific inventory and useful alternatives when a rule excludes the applicant. Do not claim to be first. |
| Remote job tools for agents | [Himalayas MCP documentation](https://himalayas.app/docs/remote-jobs-mcp) describes public search, job details, country filters, salaries and market statistics. [Left Join Jobs](https://www.leftjoin.work/mcp.html) offers values/mission-based filtering and employer profiles. | A generic `search_jobs` wrapper is already commoditised. Improve our existing REST/MCP with evidence, unresolved constraints and reliable freshness. Agent usage is not automatically human traffic. |
| FTE / pro-rata pay | [Calks](https://calks.uk/calculator/pro-rata-salary-calculator/) and [AnnualLeaveCalc](https://annualleavecalc.com/pro-rata) already calculate part-time pay. [CharityComms listings](https://www.charitycomms.org.uk/my-career/sector-jobs) visibly use both FTE and actual pro-rata figures. | Do not build another salary/hourly page: we already have one. Add actual-pay, limited-term and advert-ambiguity modes; offer the calculator from relevant jobs. A £40k FTE salary at 21/35 hours means £24k annual gross, and a six-month term means £12k total under constant-pay assumptions. |
| Fellowship finances | [APHL's stipend guidance](https://www.aphl.org/Career-Pathways/fellowships/Documents/Fellowship%20Stipend%20Estimator%20Tool%20Information.pdf) discusses relocation support, location adjustments and first-payment timing. [Its current FAQ](https://aphl.org/TPD/career-development/fellowships/program/fellowship-faqs) describes monthly payment and support funds. | Real programmes have cash-timing issues, not just an annualised stipend. Let users enter current programme terms; do not generalise one programme's payment schedule to others. |
| Fellowship discovery | [ProFellow](https://www.profellow.com/tips/secrets-profellow-database-find-fellowships/) already provides funded-opportunity discovery. [BASE's FAQ](https://www.baseresearch.org/faq) documents global remote participation; [Singapore AI Safety Fellowship](https://www.aisafety.sg/programs/singapore-ai-safety-fellowship) documents residential participation and housing/travel support. | Distinguish remote from relocation and cash stipend from covered costs. Link each criterion to the actual cohort source. A programme can recur while a specific application round is closed. |
| Deadline conversion | [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/countdown/create) provides timezone-aware countdowns, while [AoE Deadline](https://aoedeadline.com/) already supports shareable Anywhere-on-Earth deadlines. | Generic conversion has strong alternatives. Our advantage would be a verified job/programme deadline, not a new clock widget. Ambiguous `midnight`, missing zones and DST overlaps require confirmation. |
| Broad salary research | [Idealist Salaries](https://www.idealist.org/en/careers/idealist-salaries) and established nonprofit salary tools compete for this audience. | Strengthen our existing salary explorer only where comparable, source-grounded samples are sufficient. Keep currency, period, hours and sample size visible; do not publish a spurious global median. |

Competitor pages are product claims and observed features, not independent proof of their accuracy, traffic or revenue. Search inspection does not establish low competition or guaranteed ranking feasibility.

## Shared implementation contract

Build each capability once, expose a readable page plus a small versioned JSON/REST function, and selectively add the same function to our existing MCP server. The report's public aggregate is separate from licensed individual-job access. Do not weaken current API quotas or grant full-catalogue access accidentally.

For evidence tools return `supported`, `conflicting` or `unknown` per constraint; retain exact source quotes, source URL, observed/check time and the original unit. Keep source label evidence separate from explicit employer text. Work eligibility is not visa or legal advice, and residence, citizenship, work authorisation and payroll availability must not collapse into one boolean. Never label the applicant qualified from country alone.

For calculators use deterministic arithmetic. Return the formula and all user assumptions. Do not add an LLM to multiplication or infer tax, living costs, hours, payment dates, foreign exchange or reimbursement timing. A source-verified structured extraction can prefill fields; the user must confirm unclear fields. Reuse current extraction caching rather than making one provider call per field.

Do not fetch arbitrary user URLs from the server in a first release. Accept a Remote Impact job ID or pasted public posting text with an input limit. If outside URL fetching is later necessary, it requires SSRF controls, bounded redirects/body size/timeouts and explicit rules for blocked sources. Private CVs and immigration details are not needed for the first checker. Do not store pasted personal information for acquisition analytics.

A useful salary example should be available without signup; results should lead to matching jobs, not a compulsory email gate. No recurring newsletter enrollment or paid trial is implied by a calculation. Share links must omit personal finances, residence details or pasted text unless the user explicitly chooses to share it.

## Validation and free distribution

1. Prototype country checking against a fixed evaluation set of clear, restricted, conflicting, unsupported and out-of-date source cases. Audit error examples before launch, especially “remote = worldwide”, US states mistaken for countries, headquarters mistaken for hiring location, and required/preferred confusion. Keep an exact-source comparison with existing Scout results.
2. Launch the FTE extension as the lowest-maintenance acquisition experiment. Confirm all periods, fractional hours, zero/negative/non-finite values, ranges and limited-term arithmetic. The page must explain the difference between annualised rate, contract total, gross and take-home.
3. Test the fellowship cashflow prototype with several real programme terms and synthetic household budgets. Ask whether upfront cash, pay timing and covered costs change the decision. This is a usability test, not a claim that a programme is affordable for everybody.
4. Add relevant links from existing nonprofit, early-career, salary and job detail pages. Provide a small accessible embed or copyable result only after validating demand. Prepare useful examples for careers advisers and community editors; emails and outreach stay paused until separately authorised.
5. Expose agent examples on `/developers/` with explicit source attribution and human-view links. A useful answer should help a person inspect and apply; count agent calls separately from user sessions and signup/payment evidence. Do not advertise a protocol or capability that is not implemented.

Measure `tool_view`, `tool_start`, `tool_result`, `tool_to_jobs` and meaningful Scout/save/apply-click activity. Prefer a small fixed vocabulary and no raw input logging. Report authenticated product actions separately from aggregate anonymous events. Apply clicks are not applications or hires. Bot fetches are not users, and aggregate events do not form a verified sequential funnel.

Review after four weeks of actual exposure, with the observation window and small samples stated. Use result completion, relevant job exploration and useful Scout outcomes to decide which tool to improve. If impressions are absent, diagnose indexing/discovery and actual demand before building more pages. If people use a calculator but do not explore suitable jobs, revise the fit and onward path before chasing more generic visits. There is no credible guaranteed traffic or citation target at this stage.

## Explicitly defer

- PSLF calculator: still a plausible US nonprofit niche, but official-rule maintenance and employer-specific eligibility need a separate reviewed design. Do not mark a job PSLF-qualifying just because its employer name says “foundation”.
- Another generic cover-letter writer, broad remote-job API, word counter, Pomodoro timer or standalone timezone-overlap calculator: these capabilities already exist on our site or in strong competitors.
- Thousands of country/tool landing pages: generate none without distinct useful inventory and evidence of intent. Sparse combinations should remain filters, not thin indexable pages.
- Tool pages promising “AI-approved”, a guaranteed fit score, no competition or predictable traffic: unsupported.
