Gigs / AI evaluation

Paid AI evaluation work in your language

AI models need people who know a language, a country or a field to check them. We're forming a small, paid, fully remote cohort, open to people in any country, who test AI systems in their own languages and areas of expertise: checking answers for accuracy, spotting harmful or biased output, and writing the hard questions models get wrong.

This is a waitlist. The cohort isn't running yet; we'll only start once the first project is funded, and we'll email everyone on the list before then.

What the work looks like

  • Reading AI answers in your language and marking what is wrong, unclear or unsafe.
  • Writing questions and scenarios from your country or profession that models handle badly.
  • Checking translations and local facts in health, law, education and public services.
  • Remote and part-time, a few hours a week, with written instructions and examples.

Our commitments

Pay
A published hourly pay floor before anyone starts, and payment for all accepted work.
Adults only
You must be 18 or older. We check this before the first project.
No disturbing content
The first projects won't include graphic or traumatic material.
Your data
We use your details only for this cohort and delete them if you ask.

For AI labs and research teams

If you need evaluation or red-teaming in specific languages, countries or professional fields, email hello@remoteimpact.org. We'll tell you who is on the list and what we can run.
