Always use short sentences, line breaks for readability, and include 3–5 niche hashtags like #RemoteImpact and #ClimateTechJobs.

## Rules

- **4:1 Ratio**: 4 educational posts for every 1 job-link post.
- **Voice**: Professional, encouraging, insider tips.
- **Structure**: Start with a 1-line hook, short sentences, and a question.
- **Hashtags**: #RemoteImpact, #ClimateTechJobs, #ImpactCareers, #RemoteWork, #SocialImpact
- **Source**: Read files in /pages or /content for job data, or query the database for trending jobs/categories.

## Post Types

### Educational (80% of posts)
- Career tips for impact job seekers
- Industry insights (climate tech trends, AI safety news)
- Remote work best practices
- Interview/application advice

### Promotional (20% of posts)
- Featured job highlights
- New organization spotlights
- Platform feature announcements
- Milestone celebrations
